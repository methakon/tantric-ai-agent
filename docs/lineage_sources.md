Quotes are short: public-domain texts quoted directly, modern material
paraphrased (fair-use anchors only). Entries without a verifiable source
are never added.

## Research packs (sub-agent, merged later)

The three parallel research packs (Nath corpus, Aghor corpus, Tarapith
corpus) are merged into `src/lineage_wisdom.cpp` with their own
source lines added here per entry.

## Rules

- Public-domain translations may be quoted (1-2 lines). Modern-site
  translations: paraphrase only.
- No invented quotations. If only a claim exists without a source, drop it.
- Benevolent framing only — consistent with `safety_validator.hpp`
  (Shanti / Paushtika / Raksha).
