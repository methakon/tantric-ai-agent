Each subject has a reader-facing section that states what the tradition reads, followed by a compact astrological-basis box naming the mechanism, and — in the appendix — the exact scores and conventions behind it. Meaning comes before mechanics.

Interpretation & Safety Notice

This report separates three things that are easy to confuse.

DATA — Placements, degrees, dasha periods and transit instants. These are computed from the birth record with the Swiss Ephemeris and are reproducible.

TRADITIONAL INTERPRETATION — The readings in the reader-facing sections. They follow classical Jyotish doctrine — whole-sign houses, the significations of planets and houses — and are offered for consideration.

PRACTICAL FOCUS — Suggestions for attention and effort. These are general considerations, not instructions or guarantees.

This is not a prediction of events.

NO EVENT-LEVEL VALIDATION — The engine's per-year risk and mechanism readings were back-tested against 13 years of documented life events. That back-test found NO ability to distinguish event years from arbitrary years: the same verdict falls in 80.5% of arbitrary category-years, and a floor-independent rank test returned a median probability of 0.500 with 0 of 20 event years in the top decile of their own null distribution. Treat every yearly reading as traditional interpretation, not as a forecast.

No death, illness, or exact monetary loss is predicted.

Difficult periods are described as attention and sensitivity, never as certainty. Sade Sati is presented as a period of heightened emotional and family sensitivity, which is how the tradition reads it in practice.

Traditional remedies are cultural practice, not medical or financial advice. Nothing here substitutes for professional care.

Remedies

Two clearly separated kinds of advice, because they are not the same thing.

Practical action

DAILY
  • Wake and sleep at consistent times; the 6th responds to routine.
  • Keep one written account of commitments; the 2nd and 8th both reward clarity.
  • Thirty minutes of quiet before work decisions, not after.
WEEKLY
  • One evening a week without screens, to protect the 4th.
  • Review in-progress commitments once a week rather than daily.

HEALTH
