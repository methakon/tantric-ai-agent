10 · KULA DEVATA / KULA DEVI
============================

Kula Devata / Kula Devi NOT YET independently established.

No deity is inferred from planetary placements. A Kula Devata is a family
tradition transmitted through lineage and practice; it is not a derivable
function of the chart, and asserting one from planetary placement would be
fabrication dressed as tradition.

WHAT WOULD ESTABLISH IT

  • The ancestral village or locality, if the family retains any record of it.
  • Family elders' account of regular worship practice — which deity or deities are actually visited, and how often.
  • A family temple or household shrine, with photographic or documentary evidence.
  • A traditional priest connected to the family, and whether one exists now.
  • Old family records — receipts, invitations to ritual, festival correspondence.
  • Inherited ritual practices — anything passed down rather than adopted.
  • Surname and community history, ONLY where actually documented — never inferred from the horoscope.

ONCE A DEITY IS GENUINELY ESTABLISHED, the framework that would then apply is
DEITY / FAMILY CONNECTION / TRADITIONAL SIGNIFICANCE / WHEN WORSHIP IS
CUSTOMARY / PRACTICAL-ETHICAL FORM OF OBSERVANCE — with traditional and
cultural framing only. It would never be stated that a deity 'will definitely
protect' the subject.

INDEPENDENT OF THE DEITY QUESTION: service to elders, care of family-held land
where lawful, and honest dealing with family are recommended in 2026 on their
