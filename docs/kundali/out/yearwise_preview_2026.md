SECTION 1 — 2026 EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
===================================

The dominant theme of 2026 is ambition held to a slower structure. Two
time-periods and four transits operate on this chart simultaneously, and
they pull in the same direction rather than opposing each other.

The Rahu Mahadasha (2019 → 2037) is the background condition of the entire
period, and its significance here is specific: natal Rahu sits in the 11th
house in its own sign Cancer, so the house of gains, network and income is
under the Mahadasha's direct authority. Rahu's dispositor is the Moon, and
the Moon is in the 8th house — so the engine of this Mahadasha (gains) is
itself driven by the house of shared resources, obligations of others and
transformation. Gains arrive, and so do claims on them. That is the central
tension of the whole Rahu period, and 2026 is squarely inside it.

The Saturn Antardasha (2024-09-22 → 2027-07-30) is the year's active
mechanism. Saturn occupies the 1st house in its own sign and rules both the
5th and the 6th. For a Virgo lagna Saturn is a functional benefic — these
are constructive houses — but its antardasha loads the period onto the self,
the body and the personal reputation. Saturn is also the Karakamsha-bearing
planet: it is the Atmakaraka, which is why this period's lesson is about
durable standing rather than visible volume.

The single strongest transit influence is Jupiter's move from Gemini into
Cancer on 1 June 2026. Gemini is the 10th house sign — career and public
standing. Cancer is the 11th — gains, network, income — and it is the sign
natal Rahu already occupies. Jupiter crossing over natal Rahu in the house
of income is the clearest single opening of the year. The first half is a
visibility window; the second half is a monetisation window. They are not
the same window, and the year's main practical error available is treating
them as one.

Saturn transits Pisces all year — the 7th house sign — aspecting the 2nd,
4th and 12th from there. So the year's friction is located not in affection
but in money, home and cost. Transiting Rahu in Aquarius puts the 6th under
a chaotic graha: work, competition, conflict, health awareness. Ketu in Leo
sits in the 12th, the house of expense and withdrawal, and aspects the 5th,
7th and 9th.

Because Rahu Mahadasha and Saturn Antardasha both resolve through dispositors
in this chart — Rahu to the Moon in the 8th, Saturn to Mercury in the 3rd —
the year rewards conduct that is methodical and communicative, and it
punishes scale that outruns preparation.

WHAT SATURN IS ASKING FOR
The 7th house is where another person's agreement becomes binding. Saturn
transiting it asks for relationships and contracts that are explicit, dated
and durable — and it asks the same of the professional relationships that
function as contracts. The natal support here is good: the 7th lord Jupiter
sits in the 2nd in its own sign, and transiting Saturn is aspecting that
Jupiter. Saturn is testing a structure that is already sound.

WHAT SATURN CAN RESTRICT
Speed, and informal arrangements. Anything agreed verbally, anything
assumed, anything without a date attached. Saturn in the 7th traditionally
delays resolution of partnership questions rather than obstructing them —
the distinction matters, because delay is uncomfortable but is not a 'no'.

WHERE DELAY MAY OCCUR
In decisions that require someone else's agreement. Concretely: contract
signing, scope negotiation, compensation conversations, joint property or
family financial decisions. The delay is structural — the other party or
the process takes the time Saturn requires.

WHERE DISCIPLINE PRODUCES BENEFIT
The 2nd (savings), the 4th (home, documents, property records) and the 12th
(expense control) are all aspected. Discipline in those three produces a
measurable result this year, and it is the least visible part of the year —
which is exactly why it tends to be skipped.

HOW TO WORK WITH SATURN
Agree terms before emotion. Write down what was actually agreed rather
than what was intended. Assume a slower timetable than feels necessary
and plan for it rather than resenting it. Budget explicitly for the total
cost of anything promised, because the aspect to the 12th means the cost
arrives after the commitment does.

WHAT TO AVOID
Open-ended commitments accepted on the strength of rapport. Verbal
agreements about money. Property or residence transactions taken under
time pressure — the 4th aspect is the one to respect.

NATAL PLANETS AFFECTED
Rahu's aspects reach four natal bodies: Mars and Saturn in the 1st, Sun and
Mercury in the 3rd. That is the Lagna pair and the 3rd-house pair — the
self and the effort/communication axis. Traditional interpretation
associates Rahu's transit with an intensification of exactly these
placements. Mars and Saturn in the 1st already carry stamina and
impatience; adding a node on top of them tends to read as restlessness
rather than as strength. The 3rd-house Sun and Mercury are the effort and
communication pair, and Rahu there makes speech and initiative less
careful than usual.

DASHA INTERACTION
Saturn's antardasha rules the 6th, and transiting Rahu occupies the 6th
sign. So the 6th is activated by both the time-period and the transit — the
strongest double activation on the 6th in this chart's current period. Read
constructively: work, routine and health attention are where the year is
actually lived. Read defensively: it is the easiest place to accumulate
avoidable strain, because workload rises and routine gets sacrificed.

POTENTIAL BENEFIT
Rahu in the 6th is traditionally associated with success through work
rather than through inheritance — and with unconventional methods inside a
routine structure. In this chart it also supports the 3rd-house pair, so
the specific benefit is communication, initiative and self-directed work
that bypasses the standard route. Combined with the Jupiter window from
1 June in the 11th, the two are complementary: Rahu in the 6th supplies
the work, Jupiter in the 11th supplies the return.

POTENTIAL DISTORTION / CONFUSION
Rahu in the 6th with a Saturn antardasha is a known configuration for
overwork. The traditional warning is not about a single dramatic error but
about sustained load without recovery — the 6th is the house of daily
routine, and a node there distorts routine rather than occasioning one
dramatic event. The second distortion is in communication: Rahu aspecting
natal Mercury in the 3rd inflates the tendency to assert before checking.

1. Separate the decision from the announcement. Rahu in the 3rd-house
   pair pushes toward speaking first; write the decision down first.
2. Require a written specification before beginning work. This directly
