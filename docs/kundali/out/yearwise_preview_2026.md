OVERALL THEME
=============
2026 runs inside the Rahu Mahadasha (2019-08-18 → 2037-08-18) with the Saturn Antardasha, which spans 2024-09-22 → 2027-07-30 and therefore covers the whole of 2026.

Rahu is on the 11th house of this chart and Saturn rules both the 5th and the 6th while sitting in the 1st. Those three placements define the year:

• Rahu in the 11th is the house of gains, network and income. Rahu's traditional significations — ambition, unconventional routes, appetite for scale, impatience with the status quo — are therefore pointed at the area of earning and professional network rather than at, say, home or health. Traditional interpretation suggests this is a period in which ambition itself is the driving force, and that the route to gains is more likely to run through unconventional or newly-built connections than through inherited ones.

• Saturn Antardasha brings the disciplinarian into the picture. Saturn sits in the 1st house in its own sign, and rules the 5th (creative output, speculation) and the 6th (work, debt, routine, conflict). An antardasha of the lagna's own sign lord-turned-sign occupant tends to make a period feel heavier than its outward results suggest: work is real, results are slower to arrive, and the value of what is built now is in its durability rather than its immediacy.

• Because both are active together, the year is best read as *ambition under discipline*. That pairing is not inherently difficult. It is the classic structure for compounding: Rahu supplies reach and appetite, Saturn supplies patience and structure. It becomes difficult when impatience (Rahu) outruns the structure that is being built (Saturn), or when Saturn's heaviness is experienced as blockage rather than as foundations.

Set against that, Saturn transits Pisces for the whole of 2026 — the 12th from the natal Moon, which is the Uday (first) phase of Sade Sati. Jupiter moves Gemini → Cancer on 1 June and continues into Leo later in the year, so the first half and second half are not astrologically identical.
