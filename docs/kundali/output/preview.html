The engine's per-year readings were back-tested against documented life events. The design was falsifiable and it falsified the model's event-detection claim. What follows is the measurement, not a defence of it.

Why the null control is the whole argument

Method

same house-activation test applied to arbitrary category-years

1022 category-years tested.

57.9% of all 876 house-years score at or above the activation floor of 2, and the median house scores exactly 2, so MATCH/PARTIAL is the default outcome rather than a signal.

MATCH or PARTIAL — the two verdicts that look like confirmation — occur in 80.5% of arbitrary years. A rule that approves four years in five has not distinguished anything, so its per-year verdicts cannot be evidence for or against any event.

The floor-independent test

Removing that artifact requires a different statistic: rank the twelve houses by activation within each year and ask whether the event's houses rank unusually high. That is invariant to the global level shift.

For each documented event, the same house-activation rule used throughout the report was applied to the event year, then the SAME rule was applied to all other years to establish a null. 27 · Validation & Limitations

Consolidated in one place so a reader who reads nothing else still encounters the constraints.

Method limitations

Annual resolution — One state per year cannot represent a marriage and its separation four months apart, a nine-year dispute, or a period of unknown length. Three of five career events are untestable for this reason alone.

Saturating activation — Activation counts transit touches; 57.9% of house-years sit above the floor, so the base rate approaches the interesting outcome.

Mixed frames — Some transit readings are Lagna-relative and some Moon-relative. Both are defensible frameworks and they point opposite ways; the frame is named wherever a transit is stated.

No vedha — Planetary obstruction is absent, so a transit benefit is computed without checking whether another planet cancels it.

No event-level validity — The back-test found no ability to distinguish event years from arbitrary ones.

What would make a re-test meaningful

Three changes, none a threshold adjustment
  • Monthly or daily resolution. A marriage and its separation four months later must become two events. Until then, same-year pairs and multi-year periods are structurally untestable.
  • A sparse mechanism model. Activation currently counts transit touches, which saturates — 57.9% of house-years sit above the floor, so the base rate approaches the interesting outcome. A natal conjunction, or a graha owning and occupying the same house, is rare by construction and therefore testable.
  • Pre-registration. Thresholds fixed and recorded before the events are seen. That is the single strongest control against exactly the kind of fitting this validation was designed to catch.

Loosening the activation threshold to make a particular year read as a match would improve the score and prove nothing.
