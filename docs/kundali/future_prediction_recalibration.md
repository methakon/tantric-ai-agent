## 1. THE RULE THIS ESTABLISHES

The validation found **zero evidence of event-level predictive validity**
(0/20 events in the null's top decile; median rank p = 0.500; best result
0.167 where chance expects 2.0 hits).

Therefore every per-year reading in the dossier — risk levels, house
mechanisms, opportunity and attention maps, period timelines — is

> **traditional interpretation, not validated prediction.**

Nothing in the presentation may assert that a year was, or will be,
"indicated" as having contained a specific event. The engine's machinery is
real and reproducible; its *event-level discrimination* is not established.

## 6. WHAT WOULD RE-OPEN THIS

A validation that could overturn NO-GO, in order of tractability:

1. **Sub-annual resolution** — monthly or daily states. Makes 2015's marriage
   *and* separation, the 2015–2024 dispute, and the two 2026 periods testable.
   This is a resolution change, not an astrology change.
2. **A sparse mechanism model.** Current activation counts transit *touches*,
   which saturate (57.9% of house-years ≥ floor). A conjunction with a natal
   graha, or a graha owning and occupying the same house, is rare by
   construction and therefore testable.
3. **Pre-registration on a held-out subject.** Thresholds fixed and hashed
   before the events are seen. The single strongest control against
   the fitting this validation was designed to catch.

**None of these is a threshold tweak.** Loosening the activation floor to make
2005 or 2024 read as MATCH would raise the score and prove nothing — the null
shows MATCH+PARTIAL already happens in 80.5% of arbitrary years.
