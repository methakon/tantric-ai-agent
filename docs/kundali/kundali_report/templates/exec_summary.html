How to read the year-by-year sections

Each year gives a dasha, the transits in force, the houses the engine activated, and a traditional interpretation. The computation is sound; the interpretation is doctrine, not prediction. No year in this document claims to have predicted an event, and the validation section shows several cases where the engine pointed away from an event that occurred.

Nothing here is a diagnosis, a death prediction, or a financial assurance. Where the traditional material is uncertain, it says so.
