Birth-time sensitivity

Readings were re-derived across a sweep of the birth minute. Where a year changes materially across that sweep it is labelled TIME-SENSITIVE wherever it appears — five years in this range. This concerns calculation stability, not accuracy: a stable reading is still not a validated one.
