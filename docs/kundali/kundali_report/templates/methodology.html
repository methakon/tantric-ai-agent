02 · Methodology & Evidence Status

Three separate layers produce this document, and they are kept apart on purpose. Confusing them is how a computed value comes to be read as a prediction.

1 · Calculation

Placements, divisional charts, dasha periods and transit instants are computed from the birth record using the Swiss Ephemeris with the Lahiri ayanamsa and the mean node. This layer is reproducible: the same inputs give the same outputs.

2 · Interpretation

Readings apply classical Jyotish doctrine — whole-sign houses, the significations of planets and houses — to the computed chart. This is tradition, not measurement. It is labelled TRADITIONAL INTERPRETATION wherever it appears.

3 · Validation

The engine's yearly readings were tested against documented life events. They failed to distinguish event years from arbitrary years. That result is reported in full in section 14 and is not softened anywhere in this document.

4 · Practical guidance

Suggestions about routine, documentation and financial discipline are general considerations. They are not financial, medical or legal advice, and no professional consultation is displaced by this report.
