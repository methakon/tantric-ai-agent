What a NO-GO does and does not mean

It does not mean the chart is meaningless, and it is not a statement about the life it describes. It means this engine, tested this way, produced no evidence of event-level predictive validity — so its yearly readings must be read as traditional interpretation and never as validated prediction.

Three changes would make a re-test meaningful, none of them a threshold adjustment: monthly or daily resolution, so that a marriage and its separation four months later become two events rather than one; a mechanism model sparse by construction, since counting transit touches saturates; and thresholds fixed in advance on events not yet seen.
