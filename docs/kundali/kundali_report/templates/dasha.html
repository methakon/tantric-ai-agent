Reading a dasha

A mahadasha sets the background conditions for twenty years or so; the antardasha inside it decides which background is foreground; the pratyantardasha does the same at a finer grain. Nothing in that sequence identifies a specific event — the validation in section 14 tested exactly that and found no ability to do so.

Calculation detail
Balance is a fraction of the lord's own period, never of the 120-year cycle. Antardasha and pratyantardasha are reckoned from each mahadasha's own lord sequence. {{ dasha.balance }}
