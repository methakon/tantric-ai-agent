Career milestones are not validated either

It would be as much an error to test only failures as it would to test only successes. Public sources establish that the software career spans at least 2013–2026; they do not establish promotions, role changes or senior transitions. Those remain not established rather than tested-and-passed.

No year in this document is presented as having predicted a job loss or a job gain. The back-test could not identify employment events, and in the one year with a stated interruption the 10th house of vocation was not activated at all.
