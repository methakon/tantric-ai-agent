D27 (Bhreshta) is not implemented. Fifteen of sixteen vargas are computed; the sixteenth is disclosed as a gap rather than filled with an invented value.

Each divisional chart is birth-time sensitive in a different degree. D9 and D7 in particular can change with a small shift in the birth minute, so no relationship or children conclusion in this document rests on a divisional lagna alone.
