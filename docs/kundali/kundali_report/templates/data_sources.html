What was deliberately not used

  • No authenticated or private profile. A professional profile was not accessed through any logged-in session. Automated retrieval of such a profile is against its terms of service, and driving the subject's own authenticated session is not a step taken here without explicit instruction.
  • No unofficial or leaked databases. Only public API responses and public index pages were consulted.
  • No invented employment dates. Absence of a public record is recorded as absence.
Separation of evidence kinds
A value transcribed from a source is agreement with that source, not independent corroboration. "Independently verified" in this document always names the second computation.
