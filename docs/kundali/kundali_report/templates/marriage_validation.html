The structural limit

One year cannot hold two events

The first marriage and its dissolution both fall in 2015, in the same dasha, from the same 7th-house state. The engine returned two different verdicts for them — MATCH and WEAK — differing only in which house list was applied to the same annual state.

This is not a tuning failure that better parameters would fix. An annual-resolution engine structurally cannot represent a marriage and its separation four months apart as distinct events. The same limit applies to the nine-year dispute, which is scored from its single start year.

Three of the five career interruptions are untestable for the same reason. This ceiling bounds what any annual engine can be asked to validate, independently of whether the astrology carries signal.

What this rules out

  • No claim that the chart indicated the marriage.
  • No claim that the chart indicated the separation.
  • No claim about the legal dispute, which was scored from one year of a nine-year period.
  • No single-year verdict is presented as more than coincidence-consistent at best; the best result in the set sits well inside the null.
