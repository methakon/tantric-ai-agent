Kula Devata / Kula Devi

Status — not verified

A specific family deity cannot be established from planetary placements. The chart does not name one and this document does not guess. Inferring a family deity from a horoscope would be an invention dressed as a finding.

Evidence that would settle it:

  • family tradition recorded by a living elder
  • temple or shrine record
  • historical puja record or festival practice

This box is deliberate rather than missing data. An unverified item that says so is more useful than a confident guess.

Method discipline
Genealogy is treated as documentation. A chart cannot establish that two people were related, and a relationship cannot be inferred from a shared sign or a matching naksatra. Where two independent father-son links showed zero shared D12 signs, that null result was reported as a null rather than dressed up as an inherited signature.
