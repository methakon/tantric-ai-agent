No single model explains both
The 9th house — the house of qualification — is highest in 2011, the year the obstruction ended. The first-class completion year 2005 is among the weakest of the five. A model with real signal should find its successes at least as activated as its failures; this one finds the reverse.

Real-world causes are not displaced

The 2008–2011 qualification delay was, by the subject's own account, administrative: repeated applications, bureaucracy, and help from a friend. That is the cause. Any astrological reading of that period is a description of the period, not an explanation of the delay, and it does not replace the administrative reality of it.

On the two failed subjects

The 1998 failure was in two named subjects. There is no established traditional Jyotish correspondence that maps History and Geography onto particular houses or grahas, and none has been invented here to fit the outcome. Testing whether the standard education mechanisms account for the examination pressure is legitimate; inventing a subject-specific rule because two subjects were failed is not.
