## 2. THE FAILURE THAT MATTERS MORE

The repeat test is not the strongest evidence in this file. This is:

**The two failures are the best-predicted and the worst-predicted years in the
education set respectively.**

| year | outcome | H9 (qualification) | H12 (loss) | p(rank) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2005 | **FIRST CLASS SUCCESS** | 1 | 0 | **0.958** ← 2nd weakest |
| 2011 | **RESOLUTION** | **4** ← highest | 1 | 0.736 |
| 1998 | FAIL | 2 | 4 | 0.347 |
| 2021 | FAIL | 3 | 2 | 0.167 |

A model with real signal should find its successes at least as activated as
its failures. **It finds the reverse.** The year the MCA obstruction *ended*
carries the strongest 9th-house reading of the five years, and the first-class
BCA year is one of the two weakest.

Only H12 behaves conventionally: high in the 1998 failure (4), low in both
successes (0, 1). One house out of four is not a model.

## 4. VERDICT

**No repeatable signature.** The 1998/2021 pair shares three mechanisms and
one dasha lord, at the 70.7th percentile — a partial repeat that chance
explains. The education series as a whole inverts on its most direct house.

Reported as a model failure, not repaired by adjusting the house mapping.
The category → house mapping was fixed before any comparison was run and was
not revisited afterwards.
