## 1. What RED means — and does not mean

> 🔴 **RED — EXTREME ATTENTION**
>

> heightened attention to health, safety, major decisions and risk management.
>

> prediction of any specific event, and it is **never** a statement about death.

This engine cannot determine death, and nothing in this report should be read as
attempting to. No illness is diagnosed here and no financial outcome, favourable
or unfavourable, is forecast.

## 2. The distinction that matters most

### A · Astronomical / chart stability — **STABLE**

Across all seven tested birth-time variants (±15 minutes around 01:00):

- The **ascendant remains Virgo** in every variant.
- **No Lagna sign boundary** was crossed.
- **No Lagna nakshatra boundary** was crossed.
- **No relevant divisional-chart boundary** was crossed.

The underlying calculated chart is therefore **relatively stable within the
tested window**. The birth data is not in question as a result of this study.

### B · Risk-classification stability — **VARIES BY YEAR**

Individual year classifications move because Vimshottari antardasha and
pratyantardasha boundaries are sharp: the Moon advances roughly 0.15° in 15
minutes, which is enough to cross a dasha cusp in a year that sits near one.

> **"Chart stable" does NOT mean "risk flag guaranteed."**
> **"Risk flag time-sensitive" does NOT mean "birth chart is invalid."**

These are two different questions about two different subsystems, and this
project answers them separately.

## 4. On the zero GREEN count

The year-level rollup produces **no GREEN years** under the current two-family
mechanism architecture.

> **This is a property of the current aggregation design, not evidence that every
> year represents elevated real-world risk.**

The rollup takes the **worst** category across six risk categories, so a single
category reaching ORANGE lifts the whole year. The per-category GREEN capability
is retained and does function — it simply never surfaces at year level.

A year absent from the RED list is **not** thereby a safe year. Nothing in this
project classifies any year as low-risk.
