## 13. Translation issues

1. **OCR corruption in the load-bearing passages.** Both the Ch. 80 vv. 47–49 Sanskrit line (`qfa** ^ 3[fcrf«nfa>T I`, `fTfipRJT *Tc5ff JfOsfa ^ || Veil`) and the Ch. 66 contributor prose are degraded. The *English* prose of v.47 is legible and self-consistent; the Devanagari is not. **No Sanskrit transcription is offered**, because none could be verified against the image.
2. **A second translation disagrees with the blogs, not with itself.** The inspected English reads four houses. Blogs quoting a shloka with five (adding the 1st) are inconsistent with the edition I read. Which Sanskrit reading is correct **cannot be settled from this text layer alone.**
3. **Verse numbering.** v.47 is internally consistent within this edition; external sources variously say "Ch. 80", "Ch. 81", "vv. 47-48". Edition-specific numbering must be cited, not a generic "BPHS 81.47".
4. **Volume II appears to contain a different book's chapter numbering in places** — a block labelled Ch. 46 appears *after* Ch. 80 content in the stream. **Page/stream order is unreliable in this file**, which is why chapter+verse is cited rather than page number.
5. **Scope mismatch.** v.47 is written about a **woman's** horoscope and predicts **her widowhood**. Extending it to a general "is this native Manglik" verdict — the modern use — is an **interpretive expansion beyond the source.**
6. **Publication year not established.** The volume itself was not shown to carry a reliable imprint year in the inspected text.

## 14. Unresolved ambiguity

- Whether the correct shloka reading includes the **1st house** (blogs' Devanagari says yes; inspected English says no). Unresolvable from a corrupt text layer.
- Whether the Moon and Venus reference points belong to any classical text at all, or are later synthesis.
- Whether the 1944 Girdhari Lal edition settles either question — its text layer is unusable, but the **page images** are on Archive.org and could be read.
- Santhanam Vol. II publication year.
- Whether Ch. 66's totals are stated *as* rules or merely as chart arithmetic — if the totals are the primary statement and the contributor prose is a gloss, the transcription path is the wrong one.
