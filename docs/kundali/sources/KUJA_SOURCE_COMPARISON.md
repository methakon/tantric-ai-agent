4. **Scope.** Santhanam v.47 is a **woman's-chart** rule about widowhood. The general both-sexes "Manglik" test is a later expansion.

**Manufacturing a single rule from this would mean silently choosing sides in a disagreement the sources have not settled.** That is precisely what §5 forbids.

## 6. Translator / editor notes preserved

- **Santhanam, Vol. I Preface** — four-manuscript collation: Sri Venkateshwata Press Bombay; Hindi by Śyāmara Jha (Khelari Lal, Varanasi); Hindi by Devācandra Jha (Chaukambha); Hindi by Gaṇeśa Datta Pathak (Thakur Prasad). States: *"After scrutinizing critically the four manuscripts, I for reasons of more credibility have chosen the Sanskrit version rendered by Sitaram Jha. The Chaukambha version is almost the same as Khelari Lal's."* **This recension caveat remains attached to the Santhanam reading and is not dropped.**
- **Santhanam, Ch. 80 Notes to vv.47–49** — the word "Manglik" occurs **only in this expository note**, not in the verse: *"Actually the man or woman having in their nativity Mars in the houses mentioned in verse 47 are called Mangali or Mangalik."* The terminology is the translator's, not the verse's.
- **Sharma/Chatur** — no editorial notes recoverable; text layer carries no English.
